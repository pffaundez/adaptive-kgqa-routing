# Project Roles

This document defines provisional authorship responsibilities for the research project. Role descriptions record expected contributions and do not replace explicit authorship confirmation.

## Lead Author

Primary ownership:

- define the research questions and workflow-routing formulation
- design and generate the counterfactual dataset
- implement the router and quality–cost evaluation
- coordinate experiments, reproducibility, and result integration
- lead manuscript drafting

Expected CRediT contributions: Conceptualization, Methodology, Software, Formal Analysis, Investigation, Data Curation, Visualization, Writing – Original Draft, and Project Administration.

## Supervising Co-author

Primary ownership:

- provide big-picture scientific supervision
- review research positioning, scope, and claims
- conduct high-level methodological review
- support interpretation of results
- provide manuscript-level review and quality assurance

Expected CRediT contributions: Conceptualization, Supervision, Methodology, Validation, and Writing – Review & Editing.

## Co-author 2 (Prospective)

**Status:** Unconfirmed.

Proposed ownership:

- co-design and validate the KGQA workflow methodology, particularly the separation between W2 and W3
- contribute to the KGQA pipeline, including Freebase retrieval and S-expression/SPARQL generation and execution
- validate retrieval budgets and stopping rules and identify experimental confounds
- help select and implement KGQA baselines and assess reusable components
- co-own Phase A0 diagnostics for workflow complementarity, oracle headroom, exclusive wins, and failure analysis
- develop the error taxonomy across entity linking, retrieval, generation, parsing, and execution
- contribute to the Methodology, Experimental Setup, Results, and Error Analysis sections

Expected CRediT contributions: Methodology, Software, Validation, Investigation, Formal Analysis, and Writing – Review & Editing.

## Responsibility Boundaries

- The Lead Author owns the routing novelty, counterfactual dataset, router implementation, and project coordination.
- The Supervising Co-author owns global scientific supervision and manuscript-level review.
- Co-author 2 is expected to own a bounded KGQA methodology and experimentation work package rather than provide only general advice.
- The Co-author 2 assignment must be explicitly confirmed before a person is named in this document.

## Confirmation Milestones for Co-author 2

Before confirming authorship, the prospective contributor should:

- review the controlled workflow, infrastructure, and execution-contract decisions
- agree on the operational separation between W2 and W3
- identify reusable KGQA components and implementation risks
- take ownership of at least one defined experimental work package
- review Phase A0 results and contribute to the KGQA error analysis
